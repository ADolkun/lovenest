How tagging works

These totals are read live from Lovenest (read-only), counting only posted debits dated on or after Jan 1, 2026, excluding any transaction marked ignored.

  • Business / Schedule C group — any category filed under this category group rolls into the Schedule C total automatically. No tag needed.
  • #ded — add to a transaction's notes to flag a personal deductible expense.
  • #ded-charity · #ded-salt · #ded-medical — subtags split deductions by type; each exact tag is totaled separately and shown with a plain-English label.
  • Click any category or tag to expand its underlying transactions (date, description, amount).
